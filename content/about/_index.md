I am a Master's student at [Heidelberg University](https://www.physik.uni-heidelberg.de/?lang=en), and I earned my Bachelor's (BTech) at the National Institute of Technology, Calicut ([NITC](https://depcent.nitc.ac.in/physics/)). There I wrote two theses — "Introduction to Lattice Gauge Theory with Wilson Loops" and "Coherent State Path Integral Formalism for Fermions in Quantum Mechanics" — under my esteemed supervisor [Dr. Sreeraj](https://nitc.ac.in/department/physics/faculty-and-staff/faculty/3a45fbe4-3831-4589-bfdc-27d07d5dfa49).

My primary inspiration for pursuing physics is Lev Davidovich Landau (Russian: Лев Дави́дович Ланда́у); his books, and other Russian texts, deepened my passion for the subject.

This site is mostly my rantings on a handful of topics, but I hope some of it brings out ideas that make a concept or two a little easier to grasp. It is also where I record the thought processes I work through from day to day. Theoretical physics is my passion, but I also dabble in basic electronics, CAD design and a good deal of computation.

I like to think of myself as a gardener: I tend to my thoughts and nurture them until they grow into something useful. That is one reason this is the fourth iteration of the site. You can visit the third iteration [here](https://website-5m1.pages.dev/); the second ran on Ghost and the first on WordPress, both hosted on a private server that is now taken down. A few of those old articles survived and made it here.

Beyond all this, I love to lose myself in books — sci-fi, fantasy or non-fiction. Asimov's *Foundation* is a favourite, as is Frank Herbert's *Dune*. I enjoy poetry too; mine is "Forever — is composed of Nows" by Emily Dickinson. I also do a fair amount of road cycling and mountain biking, the former being the larger part of it. Check me out on [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/ashwinbalaji) and [Strava](https://www.strava.com/athletes/ashwinbalaji) to see what I am up to, and if I am out on the bicycle you can most likely track me [live](https://live.garmin.com/ashwinbalaji).

All of this might sound like me chasing after irrelevant things, or just trying out new stuff for fun. But I prefer to think of it as a fantasy novel rather than a single romance — full of world-building, tangled character arcs, sub-plots, a magic system, lore and a lot of excruciating detail that has plenty of people wondering, "when will this get to the point?" It all ties together and comes good by the end of the series. It is a lot of work, but the reward is, for lack of a better phrase, out of bounds. I like to imagine these "distractions" coming together the same way one day.

You can indulge in those distractions by following the articles or the social links here, and if you feel like it, leave a comment or just say hi. The easiest way to reach me is [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ashwinbalaji00/) — but, like most people, if I don't reply, ping me again.
