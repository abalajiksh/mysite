## 3. Hosting (Cloudflare Pages)

This website is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**, a service provided by
Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you
access the website, Cloudflare — acting as a processor — automatically collects
and processes information that your browser transmits with every request
(`GET` request):

- the IP address of the requesting device,
- the date and time of access,
- the URL / file requested,
- an approximate geolocation (derived from the IP address),
- transmitted browser headers (e.g. user agent, referrer, language).

This access data is processed by Cloudflare; the controller has only limited
influence over the specific retention period. It serves the secure and
efficient delivery of the website and the mitigation of attacks.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and
efficient provision of the website).

**Transfer to third countries:** Processing may involve a transfer to the USA.
Cloudflare, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework;
additionally, Standard Contractual Clauses are in place as appropriate
safeguards within the meaning of Art. 44 et seq. GDPR.

## 4. Server Log Files

Beyond the access data processed by Cloudflare (see Section 3), no separate
server log files of our own are kept for delivering the website. Log data
relating to the comment function is described in Section 6.

## 5. Cookies and Local Storage

This website sets **no tracking or marketing cookies**.

Your browser's **local storage** is used to save your display preference (light
or dark mode). This information remains solely on your device, is not
transmitted to the server, and serves only as a convenience feature. You can
delete it at any time through your browser settings.

**Legal basis:** Section 25(2) TTDSG (storage strictly necessary to provide the
explicitly requested service) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 6. Comment Function (Remark42)

Comments on posts use the self-hosted, open-source comment engine **Remark42**.
The instance runs on a server operated by **Hetzner Online GmbH**,
Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, in a data centre in Nuremberg (Germany)
(`comment.ashwinbalaji.xyz`).

No user account or login is required to comment. When you submit a comment, the
comment text, the timestamp, and an optional name you provide are processed and
stored; for technical reasons your IP address may be processed in the process.
Do not enter personal data into the comment text that you do not wish to be
published. Comments are stored until you or the controller delete them.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent given by submitting the comment)
and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a comment function).

## 7. Embedded Features and Libraries

All fonts, scripts, and rendering libraries are **self-hosted**; no content is
loaded from third-party CDNs. In detail:

- **KaTeX** and **Mermaid** are loaded only on pages that contain mathematical
  formulae or diagrams respectively. Rendering takes place entirely in your
  browser.
- **Alpine.js** (CSP-friendly build, without `eval()`) powers individual
  interactive posts. All calculations run locally in your browser; no data is
  transmitted to the server.

Because these resources are delivered locally, no separate transfer of data to
third parties takes place.
